The story

The game is framed by an original work of historical fiction: glass drops from a Pompeii furnace, a scratched nineteen-line grid in the back of a thermopolium, and an eruption that preserved a game mid-play for two thousand years. The full story is the landing page. The game mechanics derive from the real five-in-a-row tradition; the name and the presentation are original works.
