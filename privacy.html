Lapilli Stones — Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026

The short version

Lapilli Stones collects no data. None. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no account system of its own. Your games are stored on your device.

Solo play

Games against the computer or a friend on the same device work entirely offline. Game state and settings are saved locally on your device and never leave it.

Win/loss records

The app keeps your lifetime win/loss records. If you use more than one device, those records sync between your own devices through your iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. Apple carries that data under Apple's privacy policy; we operate no servers and cannot see it.

Online play

Optional online matches use Apple's Game Center. When you play online, Apple's Game Center service handles your player identity, match invitations, and the transfer of moves between players. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours — we operate no servers and receive no data. The only information inside a match is the state of the board itself.

What we never do

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. We embed no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network connections except those Apple's Game Center makes on your behalf when you choose to play online.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, children included.
