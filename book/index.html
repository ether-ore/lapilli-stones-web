The Reader's Shelf

For readers of the companion book

If you arrived here from the last page of the book — welcome. This page is the book's living appendix: the things paper can't hold, and the things that change after ink dries. It isn't linked from anywhere else on the site; it belongs to readers.

On the Shelf

A board you can print tonight

The full 19×19 board from Lapilli Stones, sized for a Letter or A4 page. Print it, find two kinds of anything small — coins, buttons, dried beans — and every exercise in the book is playable at your kitchen table.

Every diagram in the book — the shapes, the games, the problems — as full-colour images, one file per diagram, numbered to match the text. For study, for printing, or for arguing about Problem 13 with a friend.

Corrections

None yet. When sharp-eyed readers find errors in the book, they'll be listed here, newest first — so a printing you bought years ago stays honest. Found one? Write to lapilli-stones@proton.me.

The game itself

Lapilli Stones — the app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac where the book's author plays most of his own games. Three computer opponents, online play by correspondence, every rule variant the book teaches, and the companion book itself is coming to the app in a future free update.
