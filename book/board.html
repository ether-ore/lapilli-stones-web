Set up from a game record

Paste a game's moves in order: as the book writes them (1. K10 2. K8 … with captures in parentheses), as the app records them, or as Pente.org lists them (1. K10 - N10 2. N7 - L8 …). White moves first, as in the book. The board replays the game, captures and all, and leaves it in Play mode for the side to move.

Setup arranges a position: stones go wherever you tap, in the colour selected, and nothing is captured — for copying a diagram from the book. Play works a line out: a pair flanked on both ends comes off the board, and the colour passes to the other side after each stone. Tap the other colour any time to play the same side twice. Remove takes any single stone away, and Undo steps back through anything you did.

Your board is kept in this browser between visits. Every position also has its own link — copy it to send a position to a friend, or to come back to it later.

This board knows only where the stones are. The game itself — four computer opponents, play by correspondence, and the whole book built in — is Lapilli Stones, for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
